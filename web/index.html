    Why Factory Method is the Optimal Pattern for GlobalDocs:

    1

    Open/Closed Principle (OCP)

    Adding support for a 5th country (e.g., Peru or Brazil) requires only creating a new PeruDocumentProcessorFactory without modifying existing factory code or client batch pipelines.

    2

    Single Responsibility (SRP)

    Creation logic is decoupled from business processing. Tax calculation lives in products, while regulatory instantiation lives strictly within country factories.

    3

    Encapsulated Client Ingestion

    The client or batch engine depends only on the abstract DocumentProcessorFactory and DocumentProcessor abstractions, eliminating tight coupling.

    📄 Electronic Invoices

    Mandatory tax ID verification. Allowed formats: .pdf, .xlsx, .csv, .txt. Rejects non-accounting formats (.md, .doc).

    ⚖️ Legal Contracts

    Requires adherence to national civil/commerce codes (NOM-151 in Mexico, Ley 527 in Colombia). Allowed: .pdf, .docx, .doc, .md, .txt.

    📊 Financial Reports

    Evaluated against IFRS/NIIF and national accounting boards (CINIF, FACPCE, CMF). Allowed: .pdf, .xlsx, .csv, .docx.

    🔐 Digital Certificates

    Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) root CA trust chain verification. Allowed formats: .pdf, .txt, .md.

    🏛️ Tax Declarations

    Jurisdiction-specific annual tax form audit (Form 110 in Colombia, F22 in Chile). Allowed: .pdf, .xlsx, .csv, .txt.
